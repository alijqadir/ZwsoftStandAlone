What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help remember your preferences, measure performance, and improve the site. Similar technologies include pixels, scripts, and local storage.

Cookies we use

  • Strictly necessary: basic site functionality and form submission.
  • Performance/analytics: Google Tag Manager/Analytics (only if enabled) to understand engagement and campaign performance.
  • Advertising: Google Ads conversion tags (if configured) to measure enquiries and optimise campaigns.

We do not use cookies to sell your data.

How to control cookies

  • Browser settings: block or delete cookies via your browser options.
  • Google Ads personalisation: opt out at adssettings.google.com.
  • Private/Incognito mode: limits cookie persistence between sessions.

Third-party services

Google Tag Manager may load Google Ads or Analytics tags if you enable them. These providers may set their own cookies. Review their privacy policies for details.
